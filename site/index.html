Paper money only. Nothing here is audited and nothing here holds or moves real funds. In live mode, crypto prices come from Coinbase spot and stock prices from Jupiter's feed for the tokenized xStocks, which follows the real share price during US market hours and the on-chain token price after hours. If those cannot be reached, prices are a random simulation; the chip at the top says which. Each trader faces a house account that takes the other side. When the engine certifies an account below maintenance margin, the house closes it at the oracle price; if that is refused, the engine's own liquidation runs. Liquidation prices shown are estimates.
