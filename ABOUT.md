# About 8LACKOUT

8LACKOUT is civilian infrastructure for the moment everything goes dark. When the
grid, the towers, or the internet fail, coordination is the first thing to die.
8LACKOUT assumes it's already gone: ordinary phones and a few cheap radios become
a self-healing mesh, an on-device Claude runs the coordination with no connection
at all, and the record of what happened commits to Solana the instant any signal
returns. Offline is the default. Online is the exception. Civilians first, always.
