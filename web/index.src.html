Paper money only. Prices come live from Pyth when the page can reach it, and from a random simulation otherwise; the chip at the top says which. Stock feeds only update during US market hours. Each trader faces a house account that takes the other side. When the engine certifies an account below maintenance margin, the house closes it at the oracle price; if that is refused, the engine's own liquidation runs. Nothing here is audited and nothing here holds or moves real funds. The engine is Percolator by Anatoly Yakovenko at commit 37cb14e, Apache-2.0, compiled to WebAssembly without changes.
